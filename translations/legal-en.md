## Translator's notes

- The German pages were updated on 2026-10-09 from TMG/RStV to DDG/MStV; this translation follows the new state (§ 5 DDG, § 18 Abs. 2 MStV, §§ 7–10 DDG).
- The German Legal Notice and Privacy Policy use "wir" (we); this was kept as "we" rather than switched to the first person singular used elsewhere on the site, to stay a faithful translation.
- The current German Privacy Policy cites no GDPR articles, so none appear here. If a contact-form section is added later (legal basis for processing form data), use the format "Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR" / "Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR" and name the mail provider (Resend) as recipient.
- Terms glossary used: Impressum → Legal Notice; Datenschutzerklärung → Privacy Policy; AGB → Terms and Conditions (short link label "Terms"); Auftraggeber → Client; Designer → Designer; Auftragsbestätigung → order confirmation; einfaches Nutzungsrecht → simple, non-exclusive right of use; Verbraucherschlichtungsstelle → consumer arbitration board; Diensteanbieter → service provider; Berufsbezeichnung → professional title.
